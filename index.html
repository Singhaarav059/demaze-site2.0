FAQ

Your questions, answered with clarity

What is this AI platform designed for?

Message helps you write, plan, and think faster — drafting text, answering questions, and keeping you focused across everyday work.

Do I need technical knowledge to use it?

No. Message is built for a plain-language conversation — type or speak what you need and it takes care of the rest.

Which AI models power the tool?

Message runs on a blend of leading language models, automatically routed to whichever performs best for your request.

Is there a free plan available?

Yes — the Starter plan is free forever, with enough prompts each month to explore the core features.

Can I use this for business purposes?

Absolutely. The Pro and Lifetime plans add integrations, API access, and admin controls built for teams.

How can I get support if I have issues?

Community support is available on every plan, with priority and SLA-backed support on Pro and Lifetime.
