FAQ

Questions we actually get asked

What makes Demaze different from other development companies?

We're an AI-first technology partner, not just a development shop. Most companies focus on writing code; we specialize in systems that use AI, machine learning, and automation to solve real problems, and we stay involved as an extension of your team instead of disappearing after handoff.

How long does it typically take to develop a custom solution?

Depends on scope. Simple applications run 6 to 12 weeks. Most custom builds take 3 to 6 months from concept to launch. Complex AI platforms or enterprise systems can run 6 to 12 months. You'll get a real timeline during the initial consultation, not a placeholder.

Do you work with startups or only established enterprises?

Both. Early-stage teams get MVP development; established businesses get full digital transformation. The engagement model scales to match your size, budget, and growth stage, not the other way around.

What ongoing support do you provide after launch?

Everything from basic maintenance to a fully managed team on retainer: updates, performance monitoring, technical support, and training for your own staff when you want to bring things in-house.

How do you handle security and confidentiality?

We sign NDAs before any project discussion, follow secure development practices, and maintain data protection compliance including GDPR and CCPA. Every team member is bound by confidentiality agreements, full stop.

Got something worth building?

Tell us what it is. We'll tell you honestly if we're the right team for it.
